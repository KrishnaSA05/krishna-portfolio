About

Engineer. Researcher. Builder.

I'm an AI/ML Engineer and M.Sc. student in International Automotive Engineering at Technische Hochschule Ingolstadt, specialising in machine learning, computer vision, and sensor fusion. Fluent in English and conversational in German (B2). Comfortable moving between research, engineering, and cross-functional teams — and driven by the challenge of closing the gap between ML research and real-world deployment.
