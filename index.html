About

Engineer. Researcher. Builder.

I'm an AI/ML Engineer and Data Scientist with an M.Eng. in Electrical Engineering and Information Technology from Technische Hochschule Ingolstadt, Germany, specialising in Generative AI, Computer Vision, NLP, and predictive analytics. My work spans the full ML lifecycle — from data engineering and model architecture through to MLOps and production deployment.

Most recently, as a Workstudent at VITRONIC Machine Vision GmbH, I helped design a production-grade Agentic RAG system for intelligent traffic enforcement — combining hybrid retrieval, a LangGraph multi-tool agent, and multimodal search over camera detection data. For my Master's thesis at VITRONIC, I designed a Siamese network (ResNet + ViT) for vehicle re-identification, achieving 94% pairwise verification accuracy on NVIDIA A100 GPUs.

Earlier, I worked at Schaeffler Technologies building agentic and RAG-based tools for AI-driven scouting and real-time computer vision safety systems, and at CARISSMA Institute for Vehicle Safety Research on feature engineering and regression modelling over EV telemetry data. Fluent in English, conversational in German (B2) — driven by closing the gap between ML research and real-world deployment.
